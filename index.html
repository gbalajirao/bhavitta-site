What it does

One model for your whole financial life

From your working years into retirement — income, taxes, a home, kids' college, a pension or social security, a move abroad, and one-time events — all on a single interactive timeline, with a plain-language readiness score and one-tap what-ifs to help you decide what's next.

Life-stage timeline

Map every chapter as a stage — any of 49 curated cities (11 in the US, 38 worldwide) or one of your own, each at a Premium, Comfortable, or Modest lifestyle, plus college, retirement, and owning or renting. Gaps auto-fill so the model never breaks.

Two simulation models

Replay decades of real market history (1928–2023, nominal or inflation-adjusted) or run 10,000 Monte Carlo trials. Both give you a probability fan and your true survival rate.

One score for "am I ready?"

A single 0–100 readiness score sits above the chart, with a plain-language reason why and the changes that would move it most — retire later, spend less, or survive a bad first year. Share the result (amounts hidden by default) or set a check-in reminder, entirely on-device.

Try a what-if in one tap

Model a job loss, a market crash, retiring early or late, moving abroad, or a big expense — see the before/after instantly with direct retirement-age and spending sliders, then keep it in this plan or save it as a new scenario.

Compare scenarios side by side

One tap opens Compare with a plain-language winner line — "Moving to Bangalore at 50 lets you retire 4 years earlier" — overlaying up to three plans with success rates and closing balances together, or export Markdown for an AI second opinion.

Private by design

Everything is stored locally on your device. No accounts, no servers — your numbers never leave your phone. 21 display currencies, including Indian lakh/crore grouping.

FAQ

Straight answers before you install

The questions people ask first, answered without marketing, including what Bhavitta does not do.

Is it free? What's the catch?

Bhavitta is free on the App Store and Google Play with every feature included. There is no subscription, no paid tier, no ads, and no account. If the app helps you, there is an optional tip jar; a tip is a thank-you through Apple or Google and unlocks nothing.

Where does my financial data go?

It stays on your phone. There is no account and no Bhavitta server; every scenario, stage, and amount is stored locally. The app sends anonymous usage and crash diagnostics (which screens are used, a rounded success-rate bucket, error codes), never amounts, names, cities you typed, or your plan. The News tab downloads public feeds and market prices; nothing about you goes with the request. Details are in the Privacy section.

Does it handle India and US taxes?

It is not a tax calculator. Each life stage carries a blended tax rate you set, for example 32% while working in Seattle and 22% in Bangalore, applied to that stage's income, plus a capital-gains rate on property sales. It does not model US brackets, Indian slabs, filing status, or tax treaties. Use it to test whether a plan holds, not to compute a tax bill.

What about EPF, NPS, PPF, 401(k), Social Security, or a pension?

Account balances such as EPF, NPS, PPF, 401(k), or an IRA go into your starting corpus. A pension, Social Security, an annuity, or any other guaranteed income is entered as inflation-indexed income on the stage where it starts. The app does not model account-specific rules such as lock-ins, withdrawal limits, or required minimum distributions.

How does the simulation work?

Two models. Historical replay runs your plan through every start year from 1928 to 2023, in nominal or inflation-adjusted terms. Monte Carlo runs 10,000 trials from the expected return and volatility you set. Both produce a fan of projected balances, a survival rate, and a 0 to 100 readiness score, and a return-by-inflation stress heatmap shows how sensitive the plan is. A year-by-year ledger shows the cash flow behind any percentile path.

Does it work in rupees and for Indian cities?

Yes. Choose from 21 display currencies, including rupees with lakh and crore grouping, and 49 curated cities with local cost-of-living estimates: 11 US cost tiers and cities plus 38 worldwide, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Dubai, Singapore, and Lisbon among them. Or add your own city with your own numbers.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, in three ways. Back up every scenario to a single file and restore it on any phone. Export a scenario as Markdown to paste into an AI assistant for a second opinion. Share the readiness result as an image, with amounts hidden by default. Each goes through your phone's own share sheet, only when you tap it.

Is there a web or desktop version?

Not yet. Bhavitta runs on iPhone (iOS 18 or later) and Android phones. Your numbers do not sync anywhere, which is also why there is no account; the backup file is how a plan moves between devices.

Is this financial advice?

No. Bhavitta is an educational planning tool. Its projections are estimates based on your inputs and on historical or simulated market behaviour, not predictions or guarantees. For decisions about your own money, talk to a licensed adviser; the Markdown export is built to make that conversation easier.

How is it different from a retirement calculator?

A calculator takes one set of numbers and gives one answer. Bhavitta models your life as a timeline of stages, a city and lifestyle for each chapter, a home, college, a move abroad, retirement, and simulates the whole path thousands of times. Then it lets you change one thing, a job loss, a market crash, retiring two years earlier, and see the readiness score move.

Privacy

Your money stays on your device

Bhavitta is built to be useful without ever asking you to trust it with your data.

Your plan never leaves your phone

Every scenario, life stage, and amount is stored locally on your device. It's never collected, uploaded, or shared — that hasn't changed.

Anonymous usage analytics

The app sends anonymous, non-identifying analytics (which screens get used, which features get tapped) to help us improve it. It never sends amounts, names, cities you typed, or your plan itself — only coarse signals such as a rounded success-rate bucket, a planning horizon in years, and which kinds of life stages a plan contains. Nothing is ever linked to you.

This website counts anonymous visits and taps on its download and video buttons, with no cookies and no way to identify you, and does nothing at all if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Anonymous diagnostics

To keep the app stable, it also reports anonymous crash and performance diagnostics: error codes, rough timings, and crash categories. Never a stack trace, never your plan, and never linked to you.

Location and tips

If you tap “My location” when choosing a city, the app reads your approximate location once, on your phone, and matches it to the nearest city in its own list. It is never stored, looked up online, or sent anywhere. If you choose to leave an optional tip, the purchase goes through Apple or Google; the app only records anonymously that a tip of a given size happened, never who tipped. Tips unlock nothing.

What goes over the network

Besides the analytics and diagnostics above, the app only downloads public data: news feeds and market prices when you open the News tab, and a currency exchange rate if you ask for a refresh. Nothing about you or your plan is sent with them. On iOS, once after install, the app also asks Apple whether the install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign; Apple returns a campaign number or “no”, nothing about you.

You control sharing

Exporting a scenario or a share-image is always explicit, through your device's standard share sheet, which you initiate.

Made for adults

Bhavitta is a financial planning tool for adults. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

In short: there is no account to create, your financial plan never leaves your phone, and nothing is ever linked back to you.
