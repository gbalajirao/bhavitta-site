What it does

One model for your whole financial life

From your working years into retirement — income, taxes, a home, kids' college, a pension or social security, a move abroad, and one-time events — all on a single interactive timeline.

Life-stage timeline

Map every chapter as a stage — a city in India, a US cost-of-living tier, a move abroad (30+ curated cities), college, retirement, owning or renting. Gaps auto-fill so the model never breaks.

Two simulation models

Replay decades of real market history (1928–2023, nominal or inflation-adjusted) or run 10,000 Monte Carlo trials. Both give you a probability fan and your true survival rate.

Stress-test heatmap

A return × inflation grid shows exactly how sensitive your plan is to the two assumptions that move the needle most.

Year-by-year ledger

An amortization view of resolved cash flow — income, taxes, expenses, college, property, one-time events, and closing balance — for any percentile path.

Compare scenarios side by side

Overlay up to three plans on one chart with success rates and closing balances together — then clone, rename, share an image, or export Markdown for an AI second opinion.

When can I retire?

Bhavitta finds the earliest age you could stop working and still meet your confidence benchmark — and a goal line shows the age your median path reaches your target.

Backups you own

Back up every scenario to a single file and restore it on any phone — your plans survive a lost device without any cloud account.

Private by design

Everything is stored locally on your device. No accounts, no servers — your numbers never leave your phone. 21 display currencies, including Indian lakh/crore grouping.

How it works

Three steps to a real forecast

STEP 1

Set your inputs

Your age, starting corpus, expected return, inflation, and household size — with sensible defaults you can tune.

STEP 2

Build your timeline

Add the places you'll live and the events along the way — a home, college, retirement, a move abroad. Each stage's income and spending roll up automatically.

STEP 3

Read the outcome

See a fan of projected balances, your survival odds, your earliest retirement age, and a stress test — then tune and compare scenarios.

Watch it in action

See the whole flow in under 2 minutes

Create a scenario, add life stages, then review the Monte Carlo projections, Stress Test heatmap, and Ledger — or operate it hands-free by voice, recognised on your device.

Privacy

Your money stays on your device

Bhavitta is built to be useful without ever asking you to trust it with your data.

Your plan never leaves your phone

Every scenario, life stage, and amount is stored locally on your device. It's never collected, uploaded, or shared — that hasn't changed.

Anonymous usage analytics

The app sends anonymous, non-identifying analytics (which screens get used, which features get tapped) to help us improve it — never your financial data, never linked to you or your device's identity, and never used for cross-app tracking.

You control sharing

Exporting a scenario or a share-image is always explicit, through your device's standard share sheet, which you initiate.

In short: there is no account to create, your financial plan never leaves your phone, and nothing is ever linked back to you.
